Travel to Aspen

Aspen/Pitkin County Airport (ASE) is about 10 minutes from the venue; Denver International Airport (DEN) is roughly 4 hours by car or a connecting flight. Travel guidance, visa information, and local transport details can be found on the Aspen Center for Physics website.
